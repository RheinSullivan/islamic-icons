# Donations & Humanitarian Transparency

Islamic Icons accepts public donations with 100% financial transparency. The policy is intentionally simple and public so contributors can understand how donated funds are intended to be allocated.

## Allocation policy

- **Minimum 70%** of all funds is allocated to humanitarian aid.
- Humanitarian aid may support **Palestine relief, orphanages, low-income families, elderly care, and disaster relief**.
- **Up to 30%** may be used for framework operations required to keep Islamic Icons available and maintainable, including **server, CDN, and domain** costs.

The 70% figure is a floor for humanitarian allocation, not a target to reduce aid to. When operational costs are lower, the remaining funds can remain available for humanitarian allocation.

## Transparency reports

This document is the public reporting policy and index. Verified financial statements should be added here for each reporting period rather than inventing or estimating figures. Each report should document:

1. Reporting period.
2. Total donations received.
3. Humanitarian allocation amount and percentage.
4. Humanitarian recipient or program category, where disclosure is appropriate and safe.
5. Operations allocation amount and percentage.
6. Infrastructure expenses such as server, CDN, and domain.
7. Remaining balance, if any.
8. Supporting receipts, statements, or other verification links when they can be safely published.

## Humanitarian priorities

Palestine relief is explicitly included among the humanitarian priorities. Other supported categories include orphanages, low-income families, elderly care, and disaster relief. Allocation decisions should prioritize documented humanitarian need and preserve donor transparency.

## Governance principle

Islamic Icons does not present Google Search, an icon source, or a third-party service as a financial beneficiary. Donation funds are separate from icon licensing and provenance decisions. The same source-aware principle applies to financial reporting: identify the source, preserve evidence, and publish verified information.

# Third-party notices

## Google Material Icons

Google Material Icons are available under the Apache License 2.0. The optional importer fetches only the exact upstream SVG from the official Google repository and stores its source URL in the catalog.

Repository: https://github.com/google/material-design-icons

## Other external assets

Individual external assets retain their own creator, source URL and license in `assets/external-sources.json` and the per-icon source directory.

## Community assets

The initial community collection was supplied by the library author as SVG files. The originals are preserved under `assets/sources/community/` and the normalized catalog keeps their semantic names and categories.

## External resources

External resources are documented in `assets/external-sources.json`. Search engines such as Google are treated as **discovery tools**, not as licenses. An icon is only imported when its original page explicitly permits redistribution.

The current external-source candidates include CC0 entries discovered from SVG Repo. Verify the individual source page before publishing a copied asset.
